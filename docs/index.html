Go Hoardcore.

Replace feature creep with inventory creep. Hoardcore helps you collect sale inventory, organize product research, and keep enough evidence around to explain why buying twelve of something seemed completely reasonable at the time.

Early development.

The authenticated catalog tracker, conservative source collection, provider-neutral research import, private photo cache, watchlists, and opt-in notifications are in active development. Review the deployment runbook and source access rules before operating it.
